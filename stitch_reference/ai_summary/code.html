Overview

Photosynthesis is the foundational biological process where green plants, algae, and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy. Using chlorophyll to capture sunlight, these organisms transform water and carbon dioxide into oxygen and energy-rich glucose, sustaining nearly all life on Earth.

light_mode Light-Dependent Reactions

  • Occurs within the thylakoid membranes of chloroplasts.

  • Absorbs photons to produce ATP and NADPH energy carriers.

  • Water molecules are split, releasing oxygen as a byproduct.

filter_drama The Calvin Cycle

  • Takes place in the stroma, independent of direct light.

  • Uses carbon dioxide to synthesize G3P (glucose precursor).

  • Requires chemical energy from the light-dependent phase.

Chemical Equation
6CO2 + 6H2O + Light → C6H12O6 + 6O2
